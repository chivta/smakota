# Ukrainian food producers and brands: do they publish КБЖУ?

Track: producer/brand-owned websites (dairy, meat/poultry, sauces/canned, bread/snacks, drinks, sweets, sport nutrition, seafood snacks), plus marketplace-style producer catalogues and GS1 Ukraine registry data.

Method: `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"` against each producer's own domain (sitemap/product pages), cross-checked with WebSearch where curl was blocked or the site is a JS-rendered SPA. Every "Nutrition values shown = Yes" row below was verified by opening a real product page and finding a numeric kcal/ккал value in the HTML, not just a claim from search snippets.

## Notes

**Molokija (Terra Food)**: the only downloadable asset is a large (4.4 MB) marketing PDF of the full yogurt/curd range. `pdftotext` extraction shows fat-percentage labels ("2,1% жиру", "5,0%") and ingredient lists (склад), but never a numeric kcal, protein, fat-gram, or carb-gram figure anywhere in the document — this is a genuine dead end for КБЖУ, despite being the most complete-looking "catalogue" of the dairy brands checked.

**Nestle Ukraine**: nestle.ua returned HTTP 403 to curl on both the homepage and a page specifically named `/randd/nutrition` ("Харчова цінність наших продуктів"), which by its title should be exactly the right page — it is behind a bot-blocking layer (Cloudflare/WAF) and could not be verified with the tools available. Web search snippets describe Nestlé's global "Nutritional Profiling System" policy but give no indication that per-SKU numeric tables live on the Ukraine site.

**PepsiCo Ukraine / Lay's / Sandora**: PepsiCo's Ukrainian brand and B2B catalogue sites (lays.ua, sandora.ua, products.pepsico.ua) are all JS-rendered single-page apps. curl retrieves only the app shell (16–28 KB for the marketing sites), so no per-product nutrition markup is visible without a JS-executing browser. The only confirmed numeric value for a PepsiCo product (Lay's, 527 kcal/133 g bag) came from the third-party barcode database proe.info, not from a PepsiCo-owned domain.

**BioTechUSA storefront**: `biotechusa.com.ua` is a standard OpenCart store and does show full numeric nutrition tables per product, but its HTML `<title>` tag reads "...купити у Львові... sportshop.com.ua" — strong evidence this domain is either operated by, or shares a codebase/template with, a general sports-nutrition reseller (sportshop.com.ua) rather than being BioTechUSA's own corporate presence. Treat the data as reliable (it's copied from BioTechUSA's own packaging/facts panel) but the "official site" label is uncertain.

**Roshen**: roshen.com behaved unusually under curl — direct catalogue paths (`/ua/products`, `/ua/ua/products`) returned HTTP 404, and a plain root fetch produced a 301 redirect that curl reported as size 244 KB but did not write to disk (repeatable). roshenstores.com/catalog (the retail-store version of the site) loaded but is a JS SPA with no server-rendered product links. This is a genuine blocker, not a confirmed absence of data — Roshen almost certainly does publish nutrition facts (third-party trackers cite specific numeric values attributed to Roshen packaging), but it was not independently verified on a Roshen-owned domain this session.

**Оболонь**: needed `curl -k` because of a "unable to get local issuer certificate" error (an intermediate-CA issue, not a Cloudflare block). Once past that, both the beer-category listing and an individual beer product page (Десант) loaded fully but contain zero nutrition text — Ukrainian beer producers in general appear not to publish КБЖУ on their own sites (compare Carlsberg Ukraine and Опілля below, which are the exceptions).

**Carlsberg Ukraine / Опілля are the exception among beer producers**: both show full "Поживна цінність"/"Калорійність" tables on individual product pages, unlike Оболонь. This is worth remembering if the bot needs beer calorie data — Carlsberg-owned brands (Львівське, Чернігівське via carlsbergukraine.com) and Опілля are reliable; Оболонь-owned brands are not.

**Kyivkhlib / "Супер Тост"**: the diary's "Супер Тост Злаковый" is Kyivkhlib's toast-bread product line (confirmed via web search: Auchan/Epicentr listings explicitly credit Kyivkhlib as the producer of "Хліб Київхліб Супер Тост"). The exact "Злаковий" (grain) SKU page was not located directly, but a sibling SKU in the same product line ("Тост світлий") confirmed the producer publishes full numeric КБЖУ for this range, so it is very likely the grain variant does too.

**Favore / Фаворит Плюс naming confusion**: the diary logs "Favore Рулет з Індички Бургунд" (a turkey product), but `favore.com.ua` is broken (redirect loop, no content), and the well-documented meat producer with a similar-sounding name, "Фаворит Плюс" (mffavorit.com.ua), makes sausages under different sub-brands (Dobrov, M'yasna Lavka), not turkey rolls. These are very likely two different producers; the actual maker of "Favore" turkey products was not identified this session.

**proE (proe.info)**: this is the single most useful non-producer source found in this track — a barcode-indexed database covering a large share of Ukrainian retail packaged food with numeric per-100g/per-package КБЖУ, sourced from pack labels. Its sitemap is a Drupal-generated index of 17 pages of up to 1,000 URLs each (up to ~17,000 URLs, some of which are language duplicates and category/taxonomy pages rather than individual food items, so the true food-item count is unknown but likely in the low thousands). No robots.txt disallow was found for individual food pages, and no explicit copying/reuse license was located.

- **Уманьмясо / Юнігран**: no official website could be located via search.
- **Baker House**: no independent producer website found; likely a retailer private label, not a company with its own site.

- **Житомирські ласощі** (lasoshhi.com.ua): domain returns 404 on both root-adjacent sitemap and direct checks.
- **Optimum Nutrition**: no Ukraine-specific producer site; global site returned bot-protected fallback content, not real product pages.
- **PepsiCo brand sites** (lays.ua, sandora.ua): marketing-only shells, no nutrition content in the server-rendered HTML.
- **products.pepsico.ua**: JS-rendered SPA; individual product detail markup could not be extracted with curl.
- **Roshen** (roshen.com): catalogue paths 404, root fetch behaved unreliably under curl; roshenstores.com is a JS SPA with no extractable product links.
- **АВК** (avksweets.com): three pages fetched, all JS-rendered shells with no server-side nutrition text.
- **Оболонь** (obolon.ua): beer category and product pages load, zero nutrition content — contrasts with Carlsberg Ukraine and Опілля, which do publish it.
- **Наша Ряба / МХП** (ryaba.ua): product page is a JS-heavy SPA; no nutrition text present in the fetched HTML.
- **Епікур** (epikur.com.ua): only 4 URLs in sitemap; the one product URL tried 404'd.
- **Глобино** (globino.ua → corp.globino.ua): resolves to a WordPress corporate/news site, not a product catalogue.
- **Флагман** (e-flagman.fish): sitemap.xml returns 200 but with an empty URL list.
- **Море Пива** (morepiva.ua): appears to be a beer/snack retailer, not the producer; the diary's specific squid-snack product was not found on the site.
- **Nestle Ukraine** (nestle.ua): blocked with HTTP 403 on every URL tried, including a page whose title promises exactly this data ("Харчова цінність наших продуктів").
- **GEPIR / GS1 Ukraine**: confirmed to hold only barcode-to-company identity data, never nutrition fields.
