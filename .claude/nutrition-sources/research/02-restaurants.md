## Notes

**KFC Україна.** The nutrition PDF is linked from `kfc.ua/nutritional-values` and hosted on the site's Webflow CDN (`cdn.prod.website-files.com`), not on `kfc.ua` itself — bookmark the CDN URL directly since the page's link could change on a redesign. `pdftotext` extracted a clean table for the "burgers" section; the PDF is 5 pages so it likely also covers chicken pieces, sides and drinks on the other pages (not fully transcribed here per the brief's "don't collect per-product values" instruction).

**McDonald's Україна.** Both `curl` and `WebFetch` failed to reach `mcdonalds.com` from this environment (connection refused for curl, 60-second timeout for WebFetch) across three attempts. This is very likely bot/WAF protection rather than the site being down, since Google's own search snippets show live product pages and a nutrition-calculator URL. Treat the "yes" in the table as high-confidence but unverified — a follow-up from a normal browser or a residential IP should confirm it directly.

**Domino's Pizza Україна.** This is a clean negative: the site is a Next.js app, and the full product catalogue JSON (extracted from `__NEXT_DATA__`) only carries `price`, `weight`, `weightUnit` and text fields — no calorie/protein/fat/carb keys exist anywhere in the payload for the pizza category. This is strong evidence, not just an absence of visible text.

**Sushi Master.** Important distinction: the numbers exist in the page's data but are not shown to a human visitor anywhere I found (no "ккал" label rendered near them) — a bot/importer reading the embedded JSON would get real values, but a person reading the page would not. Worth flagging in case this pattern also applies to visually-empty sushi sites like Багато Лосося (checked and found genuinely absent, not just hidden) and Budusushi.

**Пузата Хата, Хінкальня, Glovo, Bolt Food.** All four serve their real menu content only after client-side JavaScript execution (React/ChoiceQR-style single-page apps). `curl` gets an near-empty HTML shell in every case. This is a rendering limitation, not a Cloudflare block — a headless-browser fetch (e.g. Playwright) would very likely surface real content, including possibly nutrition data, and should be retried with such a tool.

**Yaposhka.** The `yaposhka.kh.ua` homepage links to `yapiko.ua` for its actual menu ("Yapiko" not "Yaposhka") — this may be a rebrand, a franchise partner, or a mismatch between the diary product name and the actual current chain name. Needs a human check before relying on it.

**Aroma Kava, Mafia, Pizza Day, Premium Butcher, Шаурмен, Aroma Kava.** These returned a full, real HTML page (not a JS shell, not blocked) with genuinely zero calorie/protein keywords anywhere — a stronger negative than "couldn't check."

**Мама Манана.** The presence of nutrition-related i18n keys in the page bundle (`proteinsAmount`, `carbohydratesAmount`, `kcal`) without populated values suggests the underlying ordering platform (looks like a shared e-commerce/delivery template) supports a nutrition field that this particular business has not filled in for the items checked — worth spot-checking a few more product pages (especially the frozen retail khinkali line, since the brief notes those are also sold frozen and might carry a nutrition label required for packaged retail food).

**Third-party calorie aggregators (tablycjakalorijnosti.com.ua, run.kyiv.ua).** These are not official chain sources but independently maintain per-dish kcal/БЖВ pages for at least McDonald's, KFC, Domino's, Sushi Master and Puzata Hata (confirmed via targeted searches returning individual dish URLs like `/stravy/pitsa-dominos`, `/stravy/rol-nizhnyy-z-kurkoyu-sushi-master`). For any official source that turns out to be a dead end (Domino's, Pizza Day, Mafia, etc.), these aggregator sites are a plausible fallback, though their own sourcing/accuracy was not verified.
