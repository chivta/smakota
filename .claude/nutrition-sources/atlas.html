The sources that do the heavy lifting

Most of the diary resolves through these. The full catalogue below lists the rest, including the chains that publish nothing.

Packaged food and Сільпо own brands

Сільпо product API

Per-100 g КБЖУ and barcodes in public JSON, no key. A plain name search matched 19 of your diary entries, from Snickers to Ходорівський тушонка.

Novus, METRO, Auchan and 18 more chains

Zakaz.ua stores API

One API behind 21 retail chains, with a nutrition_facts object on each product. It matched 15 diary entries, including Яготинський айран. Its terms forbid copying without consent.

Barcodes with a clean license

Open Food Facts

4.7 million products, 7,286 of them tagged Ukraine, downloadable in full under ODbL. Coverage of Ukrainian goods is patchy: one Молокія barcode resolves to an entry named "MIKC" with no nutrients.

Ukrainian packaged goods by barcode

proe.info

10,092 Ukrainian product pages with КБЖУ, ingredients and E-numbers. HTML only, no stated license. Snickers, Торчин ketchup and Lay's from your log are all there.

Settling disagreements

Producer sites

Яготинське, Комо, Чумак, Торчин, Київхліб and Козуб print label values on their product pages. Use them as the tiebreaker when retailers disagree.

Fast food

KFC and McDonald's PDFs

Both Ukrainian franchises publish official nutrition PDFs. KFC's covers the wings and bucket pieces in your log. McDonald's runs 22 pages and was created in July 2026.

Home cooking and generic dishes

CIQUAL, Swiss table, USDA FDC

Free downloads under open licenses. USDA has borscht and pigeon, CIQUAL has kebab sandwiches and roast chicken, the Swiss table has soy sauce and hot dogs.

The data you already log

FatSecret Platform API

The free tiers return US data only. Ukrainian entries like Яготинське need the paid Premier edition, priced on request.

Method and caveats

How this was put together

Seven research agents worked in parallel, one per area: supermarkets, restaurants, out-of-home food, producers, open databases, calorie apps and marketplaces. Two follow-up passes went after the retailers and chains the first round could not reach. Agents opened real product pages, sitemaps, PDFs and API responses with curl and recorded whether numbers were actually present, not just promised.

I re-ran the claims that mattered most by hand. The agents contradicted each other twice. One reported that Сільпо's API has no nutrition because it only read the search results; the product detail endpoint returns full КБЖУ. Another reported no kcal on Львівські круасани; the menu page shows 31 values per 100 g.

Before building on any of this, keep these in mind:

  • A search API's first hit is not a match. Сільпо's top result for "Лимонад" was Fairy lemon dish soap, and "Яйце" returned butter. Match on barcode where one exists, otherwise score brand plus name.
  • Sources disagree on values. Чумак sweet chili sauce is 170 kcal on chumak.com and Сільпо, 168 on Open Food Facts and 116 on a 225 g Zakaz.ua listing.
  • Your FatSecret entries mix Ukrainian and Russian spellings: Яготинский, Лагидный, Своя Линия. The retail APIs index Ukrainian names only.
  • Cloudflare blocks scripted access to АТБ, Rozetka, limo.ua and Fineli. Russian-hosted calorie sites never completed a connection. Rows for these rely on search snippets.
  • Access is not permission. Zakaz.ua and tablycjakalorijnosti.com.ua forbid copying. Open Food Facts, USDA, CIQUAL, CoFID and the Swiss table allow reuse under stated licenses. Most retailer and producer sites state no license at all.
  • The Soviet Skurikhin tables and the Russian calorie sites sit under dead ends. The scans have unclear copyright, and they are Russian-origin data.
